Combat log sync

When syncing your combat log, during an encounter you will send and receive overall combat statistics with the group. This will include, per player, the total damage done, time engaged, dps, heals, and hps.

At the end of an encounter, you will sync with the group by transmitting and receiving the combat logs for the encounter.
